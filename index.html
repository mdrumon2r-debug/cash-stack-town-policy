Cash Stack Town - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Cash Stack Town ("the game") is published by Md. Mosharaf Hossain. This policy explains what information is used when you play.

Information we collect

We do not ask for your name, email or any account. Your game progress (level, coins, shops, settings) is stored only on your device.

Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use device information such as the advertising ID, IP address and app usage data to show ads and measure their performance. Where required, you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown. You can reset or opt out of the advertising ID in your Android settings. Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the game deletes all progress stored on your device.
